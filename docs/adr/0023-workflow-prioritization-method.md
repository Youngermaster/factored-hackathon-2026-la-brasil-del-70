# 0023: Pre-registered weighted scoring for workflow prioritization, with a labeled proxy while human labels are pending

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-26

## Context

The brief asks for "a problem supported by data": contact reasons, demand patterns, data quality, and constraints used to prioritize the workflow. The scope (four workflows) is already a human decision, so the analysis must order the build and depth work and class sub-intents, not select workflows. Three facts from profiling constrain the method: the contact reasons are six coarse values, so only one of four interaction mappings is unambiguous; transcripts are two balance-question templates whatever the topic, so they cannot label three of the four workflows; and the human labeling task the prompt defines (automatable share) had not started when the scores were needed.

## Considered options

1. **Narrative prioritization.** Read the tables and argue an order. Fast, but open to choosing the story after seeing the numbers, and hard to reproduce or challenge.
2. **Pre-registered weighted scoring with human labels only.** Commit the criteria, weights, and formulas first, and leave the automatable share empty until humans label. Honest, but the score cannot be computed now, and for three workflows it may never be computable from transcripts.
3. **Pre-registered weighted scoring with a labeled structured proxy for the automatable share**, replaced per workflow by human labels once enough matching items exist, plus weight and mapping sensitivity.
4. **A learned model of automatability** (for example predicting first contact resolution). Needs labels the data does not have, and would add a model the phase does not need.

## Decision

Option 3.

- Criteria, weights (the prompt defaults: demand 20, pain 25, automatable share 20, harm inverse 10, data support 15, demo depth 10), formulas, rubrics, data support items, the ordering rule with its tie margin, and the sub-intent classing rule live in `data_platform/analysis/scoring.yaml` and `docs/analysis/workflow-scoring-preregistration.md`, committed before any score was computed.
- The automatable share is the adjudicated human share of items whose text matches the workflow once at least 75 such items exist; until then it is the share of historically simple contacts (resolved at first contact, not escalated, no follow-up), labeled "proxy" in every table. Machine pre-labels live in a separate file with `review_status=pending` and are never read as labels.
- Three mapping scenarios (primary, strict, alternative) are fixed in the mapping file and scored alongside; each weight is moved by plus and minus 5 points and renormalized.
- Every number is regenerated by `make analysis` from the warehouse; costs come from a YAML file whose values are marked as assumptions and never enter the score.

## Consequences

- The order is reproducible and cannot be tuned to the results without a visible new pre-registration version.
- Sensitivity shows which conclusions are robust (first and last place) and which are not (the middle two are within the tie margin), and the strict scenario states the breadth risk in numbers.
- The proxy measures historical simplicity, not what the system can do; the reports say so, and the decision document names the label results that would change the order.
- Harder: rubric values (harm, demo depth, capability) are judgments. They are written down with reasons, but a reviewer may disagree with them, and changing one means a new pre-registration version.
- Transcripts cannot provide router training data; phase 10 must author and label its own utterances.
