summary: Dispute reasons the assistant can record.
---
The assistant can record disputes for these reasons: a transaction you do not recognize, a duplicate charge, an amount different from the one agreed, a product or service you did not receive, an ATM withdrawal where the cash was not dispensed, and a charge for a subscription you already cancelled. Any other reason is reviewed by a person from the team.
