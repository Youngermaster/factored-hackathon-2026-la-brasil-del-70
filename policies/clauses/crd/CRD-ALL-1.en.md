summary: What is reported about a card's status.
---
We can tell you whether each of your cards is active, blocked, suspended, or closed, and its expiry date when the records hold it. If you have several cards, we will ask which one, by its type and last digits. A declined purchase is reported without its reason, because the records include no table of response codes.
