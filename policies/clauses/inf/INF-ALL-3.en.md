Your application is recorded for review by a person from the credit team, who will contact you within {review_contact_business_days} business days. Recording it is neither an offer nor a decision, and it moves no money. You can check its status here: submitted, under human review, withdrawn, or closed, and you can withdraw it while it is still open.
