summary: What happens after a dispute is recorded.
---
After your dispute is recorded you will receive a case number. The review team examines the transaction and may ask you for more information. You can check the case status right here: opened, in review, escalated, resolved, or rejected, together with the committed response date. Recording the case does not guarantee a refund or a provisional credit.
