summary: Requests the assistant does not handle, and what happens to them.
---
The assistant does not make transfers or payments, does not open or close products, does not change your personal details, does not give legal, tax, or investment advice, and does not make credit decisions. If your request is outside what it can do, it will say so plainly and, where appropriate, offer to put you in touch with a person from the service team.
